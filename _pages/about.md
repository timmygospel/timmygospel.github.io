## Hi, I'm Timmy Gospel

I'm a developer, designer, and writer exploring the intersection of **machine learning**, **AWS**, and clear technical communication.

I built this site for two reasons: to prove to employers that I genuinely understand ML — not just surface-level buzzwords — and to force myself to understand topics deeply enough to explain them clearly.

If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough. Every article here is my attempt to earn that understanding.

---

## What You'll Find Here

This site covers **machine learning from the ground up** — concepts, intuition, real-world applications, and AWS implementations.

I write in four layers on every topic:

- **Concept** — what it is, explained simply
- **AWS mapping** — how it connects to SageMaker, IAM, S3, and the broader AWS ML stack
- **Exam angle** — what the AWS ML Specialty exam tests on this topic
- **Practical angle** — when and why you'd actually use it

The goal is content that is useful both as revision material and as a portfolio of genuine understanding.
